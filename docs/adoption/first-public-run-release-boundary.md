# TARMAC First Public Run Release Boundary

This boundary closes `TARMAC-PF-05`.

TARMAC fixture-backed tests, seed schemas, CLI help, and example artifacts prove
implementation behavior. They are not a cited aviation-network finding. A public
TARMAC result remains scoping evidence until the release record proves the
source-backed run and review outcome.

## Promotion Rule

No TARMAC artifact may be promoted as a cited aviation-network finding,
engineering study, airspace or procedure design, environmental review, slot
allocation, route award, procurement plan, intervention selection, service
adequacy finding, delay finding, capacity finding, access finding, airline or
airport action, regulatory position, advocacy brief, or FAA, airport-authority,
airline, or ICAO endorsement unless the release record names:

- bounded airport system, market, route, or access question;
- source manifest path and regeneration command;
- source owner, vintage, scale, and market;
- demand basis and weather or operating basis;
- corpus artifact, tier/SLA artifact, scale-filtered gap artifact, and findings
  report;
- evidence labels, residuals, null results, and held claims;
- allowed public claim and blocked public claims;
- dispositions from the seven parliament roles;
- dispositions from the three editorial roles;
- downstream owner acceptance for the specific use.

If any field is missing, the artifact remains fixture-backed or scoping
evidence only.

## Required Review Roles

Public release must record dispositions from Aviation System Planner, Airport /
Civil Engineer, Operations & ATC Reliability Officer, Aviation Economist,
Regional-Access Advocate, Environmental & Community Advocate, Slot &
Fortress-Hub Realist, Citation Auditor, Scope Keeper, and Numeracy Checker.

## Current Status

The Rust workspace and CLI are fixture-backed and useful. The first public
cited corpus run, source manifest, tier/SLA run, scale-filtered gap, findings
report, and role review remain the next publication milestone.

`REQ-009..011` remain `pass_with_risk` for public release until a first cited
run records the boundary evidence and role dispositions above.
